CHAPTER 08 · R05 · 90 MIN

Mixed Timing & a Personal Repair Plan

混合限时、证据复盘与个人计划

A 34-minute original mixed set followed by evidence review, targeted repair, exam readiness and an individual next-step plan.

Learning goals / 本课目标

  • Maintain a workable pace on a shortened mixed set.
  • Distinguish explained success from a correct guess.
  • Repair one error through a changed-case test.
  • Plan further practice using appropriately labeled and authorized sources.

1. Complete Q21–Q40 in 34 minutes

Open the original mixed set

Work independently on all 20 questions. Record confidence and any return point. This shortened mixed set is not a complete 70-question AP multiple-choice exam and cannot predict an AP score.

教师支持：0–34分钟整段留给独立答题。34分钟近似练习读题节奏，但题量与覆盖均不等于完整考试；不要用比例换算AP等级。

2. Review evidence for 26 minutes

For each missed or guessed item, identify the first unsupported inference. Record the condition, trace step, numerical calculation or stated policy that decides the answer. Explain at least one tempting alternative, not just the correct letter.

RecordUseful evidence
Original choice and confidenceWritten before feedback
Decisive factA specific condition, value, path or permission
Why my alternative failsA contradiction or counterexample
Transfer checkA changed case with a predicted result
教师支持：34–60分钟按证据复盘。正确但不会解释的题仍需补；自信错误往往是概念误解。先修两类高价值问题，避免把所有时间用于抄完整解析。

3. Use ten minutes for one changed-case repair

Choose a case that matches your error pattern. Predict before expanding; then change one condition and solve again.

Code case

total starts at 0. In each of three repetitions, it is reset to 0 and then increased by 2. DISPLAY(total) runs after the loop. What is displayed, and what single change would make it accumulate across repetitions?

Data case

An original file is 2,000 bytes and the compressed file is 1,500 bytes. What percentage was saved, and what percentage of the original size remains?

Show answer and reasoning / 展开答案与解释

Code: output 2. Keep the initial total ← 0 before the loop, but remove the reset inside it; then three additions produce 6. With zero repetitions, that repaired version displays the initialized 0. Data: 500 bytes saved; 500 / 2,000 = 25% saved, while 75% of the original size remains.

教师支持：两例是新复习微例，挑与个人错因相关的一个做到底。代码修复保留循环前初始化，不能删掉全部初始化。压缩题必须区分节省率与剩余占比；再改一个大小验证。

4. Separate exam readiness from content repair

Use the next ten minutes to verify practical arrangements: your school’s exam details, permitted materials, access to the current official exam preview, and the status of your submitted PPR through your school. Use official sources for changes.

Official Bluebook preview guidance · Current exam information

A preview helps you learn the interface. It is not a scored practice exam. Any school registration, submission or PPR-printing issue goes to the teacher or AP coordinator; a course quiz result does not confirm those processes.

教师支持：70–80分钟处理实际信息核对，不突然开一套新题。考试时间和地点以学校通知为准；本课记录不冒充官方报名或PPR收件确认。

5. Finish with an executable personal plan

Use the final ten minutes to select two repair actions and one full-length practice opportunity. Name the source, the date, the time needed and the evidence you will check afterward. Match the action to the actual error pattern.

ActionA specific plan
Repair a conceptChoose one weak distinction and test it on two changed cases.
Repair executionChoose one short trace and explain every state transition.
Practice exam conditionsAsk your teacher to assign an available authorized full paper; label any shorter public set accurately.

Exit check: name one action you can complete next, and what observable result would show it worked. A higher repeat score alone is not enough if you remember the answers.

教师支持：最后10分钟产出可执行计划，不写“再多练习”。可用官方公开FRQ做完整书面题组；完整选择题卷依学校教师权限安排，不能把本章20题称完整模考或把受限卷公开上传。

After class / 巩固

  • Complete the two dated repair actions in your plan and keep the changed-case evidence.
  • Arrange an authorized full multiple-choice paper through your teacher if available; do not relabel the 20-question set as a complete exam.
  • For another written-response attempt, use an unused official public paper with your own permitted personal material and its year-specific scoring guide.

打印复盘记录单

教师课后复盘与分层支持
  • 20题34分钟是缩短混合练习；不等于70题120分钟完整选择题卷，不作AP等级预测。
  • 复盘优先自信错误及猜对题；每题保留原答、自信度、决定性证据、反例与新测试。
  • 十分钟补练只修一个具体机制，避免“看了解析”替代重新预测与验证。
